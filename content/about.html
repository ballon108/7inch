About

A crate under a single light.

7inch.rare releases limited 7" records in two series. Every title is a small, numbered run: when it is gone, it is gone.

My Philosophy

90s Hip Hop & R&B, one episode at a time. Each record pairs two songs that belong together: well-known classics and cuts only diggers carry in their record bag, re-edited and pressed on 7" with its own label colour.

Lowrider Anthems

West Coast and G-Funk only, re-edited for the boulevard and released in full-colour picture sleeves. The soundtrack of chrome, hydraulics and slow rides down palm-lined streets.

How it works

  • Small editions, stock shown live. First come, first served.
  • Pre-orders are shipped together as soon as the run arrives.
  • We ship worldwide from Germany.
  • Record shops and distributors can order wholesale from a partner account.
